How procurement runs — and how you stay in control

Zoho CRM is the backend system of record — where Jefferson and Kyle do the work. procurement.rentstayable.com is your read-only window to monitor and approve. You don't have to log into Zoho to see what's happening — but you can open the CRM directly anytime you want. Either way, every approval is recorded in Zoho, under your name.

Why it's built this way

Read-only by design
The portal can't change anything. It's a clean window — zero risk that a glance becomes an accidental edit.
Approvals are provably yours
Because you approve in Zoho from your own seat, every spend decision is attributed to you in the audit trail — not to a service account.
Lowest landed cost wins
Quotes are ranked by freight- and duty-loaded cost, so the cheapest sticker doesn't masquerade as the cheapest deal.
Optional — automated notifications

Nobody has to remember to check. We can wire workflow-driven alerts so the right person is pinged the moment something needs them — no manual chasing.

YouPortal & email
