03

Where they really diverge — the bid/quote layer

same job, different center of gravity

Both capture "Vendor 1 / 2 / 3 with their own status & terms" — which answers Rob's "track where we are with each contact" complaint. The split: our subform leans contact-relationship; Rob's Quotes leans commercial comparison, and adds the landed-cost math neither current option computes.

Current — Contact_Tracking subform

Inline rows on the item. Lighter; relationship-oriented.

  • Contact (lookup) + Contact Name fallback
  • Email · Phone · Role
  • Outreach Status (color-coded lane)
  • Last Contact · Next Step · Next Step Date
  • Quoted Unit Price · MOQ · Lead Time
  • Incoterm · Sample Status · Notes
Can't: per-row file attachment · per-row Kanban · per-row reporting · time-triggered per-vendor reminders · landed-cost math

Rob — Quotes module

One record per offer. Heavier; comparison-oriented.

  • Quote Status · Date Received · Vendor + Item lookups
  • Unit Price · Currency · Order Qty · MOQ · Price Breaks
  • Freight · Duty/Tariff · Inspection cost
  • Total Landed Cost + Landed Cost/Unit (formulas)
  • Ship Mode · Port of Origin · Sample Lead Time
  • Spec Match · Material · Dimensions
  • Certifications (multi-select) · Spec Sheet (file upload)
  • Warranty · Risk Notes
Can: per-quote spec-sheet PDF · Kanban by status · grouped report sorted by landed cost · award workflow. Lacks: the per-contact Email/Phone/Next-Step relationship fields (those sit on Vendor).
04

What Rob fixes · what conflicts · what to flag

➕ Gaps Rob's spec fixes

  • Landed cost math — unit×qty + freight + duty + inspection → per-unit comparator. This is our Stage 3 "Level"; neither current option computes it.
  • Per-quote spec-sheet upload — each vendor's quote PDF on its own record.
  • Certifications (BIFMA/CARB/Fire/ANSI) structured — absent in current.
  • Kanban + grouped report sorted by landed cost — the real comparison view.
  • Award = one Quote record, structurally enforced (cleaner than our Account lookup).

⚠ Conflicts with current

  • Native Vendors module — current keeps Vendors = Accounts behind the firewall (21 live). Swapping loses the firewall that separates Procurement / Non-Profit / Vendor-Selection.
  • Item on Deals/Potentials — Deals is reserved for Kate's Non-Profit Placements pipeline; putting procurement there re-collides them. (His custom-module option avoids this.)
  • Loses subform's relationship fields — Email/Phone/Next-Step per contact unless re-homed on Vendor.

◆ Flags to confirm

  • Property "2900" in Rob's picklist is not in our 8-property canon (4645, 2295, 6802, 812, 5399, 2535, 44199, 8700). New property or typo? Not invented.
  • Caution #1 is wrong for us — "custom modules need Enterprise" is contradicted by our live Procurement_Items custom module on Professional. Capability proven; only verify the custom-module count cap.
  • Currency caution is right — matches our USD base; quote in USD or add a manual USD field.

06

Recommended synthesis

take the good half · reject the two collisions · route the fork to Rob
  1. KEEP  Procurement_Items custom module — don't move to Deals (protects Kate's Non-Profit pipeline).
  2. KEEP  Vendors = Accounts behind the firewall; add Rob's 5 vendor fields to the Account vendor layout instead of standing up native Vendors. (This is the one genuine fork — Rob's call.)
  3. TAKE  Build the Quotes child module (= evolved Vendor_Bids) instead of the subform — its per-quote spec-sheet upload, landed-cost formulas, Kanban, and grouped report are now requirements, not nice-to-haves.
  4. TAKE  Point Awarded Vendor → the Quote record, and build Rob's award-stamping workflow.
  5. CONFIRM  Resolve Property 2900 before any picklist; ignore the Enterprise caution (verify only the Professional module-count cap).
