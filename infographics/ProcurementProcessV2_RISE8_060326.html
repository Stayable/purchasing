Overseas Procurement · Zoho CRM System of Record

How we source & approve, end to end, in three modules.

Every item we buy overseas runs the same five-stage path — from spec, through competing quotes leveled on true landed cost, through a Florida fit-check, to a recommendation Rob approves. Here is the whole machine on one page.

01

The three modules

One item collects many quotes; each quote names one vendor.

Read it left to right: the Procurement Item is the thing you're buying and the center of gravity. The competing offers attach to it as Vendor Quotes. Each quote points to a supplier in the Vendors directory. The winner gets stamped back onto the item.

03

The two rules that decide it

A quote can be cheap and still wrong. These gates catch both.

Florida validation

Stage 4 — every item must survive the Florida operating environment.

💧
Humidity & moldfinishes, adhesives, substrates
🌊
Salt-air corrosioncoastal property hardware
🌀
Hurricane / impact codeexterior & structural items
⚡
120V · UL / ETLno overseas-only electrical

Fail any criterion → the item is rejected or sent back to re-spec. It cannot advance to Recommend.

Landed cost is the spine

Stage 3 — sorting on unit price is misleading for overseas buys.

Landed Cost / Unit =
  (Unit×Qty + Freight + Duty/Tariff + Inspection)
  ÷ Order Quantity

A cheaper unit with worse Incoterms, a longer lead time, or unvetted specs can land higher and riskier. The formula field forces apples-to-apples — that's the whole point of the comparison view. Quotes standardized to USD.

04

Approval, PO & the paper trail

Spend tier sets who signs and what's required.
UNDER $25K

COO

COO approves.

$25K – $100K

CEO + sample

CEO approves; physical sample required.

OVER $100K

CEO + IC

CEO + investment committee, sample, pre-production inspection.

Purchase orders

PO numbers are tracked on the item in PO_Number + PO_Status across every channel (Alibaba confirmation, Amazon / Home Depot numbers). For office-supplies & linen, Jefferson generates the PO from his template and attaches the PDF. No native PO / Quotes / Products modules — that suite is the sprawl that killed v1.

Where the decision lives

The recommendation and the final ruling are written in Decision_Notes on the item — never in email. Spec sheets, leveled cost models, FL checklists and signed POs attach to the record (or per-quote spec sheets to the quote). One item, one timeline, one source of truth.
